# Decision Memo Template

Read before drafting the memo. Omit sections that don't apply (such as
`Stakeholders` on a solo decision).

````markdown
# Decision: {one-line decision}

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Draft | Decided | Revisit
- **Decision window:** {deadline or "soft"}
- **Reversibility:** Reversible | Mostly reversible | One-way door

## Context

{2–4 sentences: what is being decided and why now.}

## Evidence ledger

| Claim | Status | Source or verification needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ... | Verified fact / User belief / Assumption / Unknown | ... |

## Options

### Option A — {name}

- **Summary:** {what it is}
- **Upside / Downside / Cost:** ...
- **Must be true / Weakest assumption:** ...

### Option B — {name}

{Same concise shape; always include a "Do nothing / status quo" option.}

## Stakeholders

{Optional: who has a stake, whether buy-in is needed, and known sentiment.}

## Contrarian pass

{Steel-manned alternatives, hidden assumptions, sunk costs, second-order
effects, and reversibility check.}

## Recommendation

**{Option name}.** {2–4 sentences: why this option, accepted trade-offs, and
whether this agrees with or challenges the initial gut call: "{gut call}".}

## Kill criteria

Revisit or abandon if any of these become true:

- [ ] ...

## Open questions

{Unanswered items affecting the decision, or "None".}
````
