# AI Patterns to Remove

Read before editing. Strip AI tells without distorting meaning, technical
accuracy, or intended tone.

## Content and phrasing

- **Puffery & inflated stakes:** "pivotal moment", "testament to", "enduring
  legacy", "groundbreaking", "game-changing", "redefines the landscape".
- **Superficial `-ing` tack-ons:** trailing clauses claiming significance
  ("..., highlighting the importance of...", "..., underscoring how...",
  "..., paving the way for...").
- **Vague attribution & hype:** "experts say", "industry reports", "observers
  note" without a real source; promotional travel-brochure adjectives.
- **Cliché contrasts & padding:** "It's not just X; it's Y", "More than just
  ...", forced rule-of-three lists, synonym cycling (calling the same thing
  three names in three sentences), and filler transitions ("Additionally",
  "Moreover", "Furthermore", "In conclusion").
- **Fluff buzzwords (when used metaphorically):** "delve", "tapestry",
  "vibrant", "intricate", "garner", "foster", "showcase", "underscore",
  "align with", "navigate" (for "handle"). *Do not ban literal domain terms*
  such as `harness`, `primitive`, `API surface`, `vector`, or `ecosystem` when
  they are the accurate technical words.
- **Chatbot artifacts:** Sycophantic openers ("Great question!", "Certainly!"),
  generic upbeat closers ("The future looks bright", "Let me know if..."),
  and meta-commentary ("In this section, we will...").

## Cadence and formatting

- **Mechanical rhythm:** Uniform sentence lengths, stacked negative/parallel
  structures, and overused em dashes, parentheticals, or mid-sentence colons
  used as a crutch—keep normal punctuation where it reads naturally.
- **Template formatting:** Every bullet opening with **Bold Label:** followed
  by a colon, decorative emoji headers, or bolding every other noun phrase.
- **Hedging stacks:** "could potentially possibly", "it is worth noting that",
  "it is important to remember that". State the claim directly with its real
  caveat.
