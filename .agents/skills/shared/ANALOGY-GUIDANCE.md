# Analogy Guidance

Use an analogy when it gives an abstract idea a familiar foothold. Prefer
something the learner has shown they know; don't guess their interests.
Map the relevant parts, name where the analogy stops matching, then
restate the precise concept. Check understanding with a fresh example
of the real concept—not just whether the analogy made sense.
Skip it when a direct example or diagram is clearer, or when it blurs a
technical constraint.
